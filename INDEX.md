# Footage library

Contact sheets for every video in the Drive `Footage` folder, subfolders
included. **82 clips**, 18.3 MB of sheets.

This is phase 1: sheets only, no video. The point is that the hub picks the
shots worth having from these, and phase 2 transcodes just those - roughly
fifteen - rather than mirroring 200 MB nobody asked for.

Each sheet is a 3x2 grid sampled at 5, 25, 45, 65, 85 and 97 percent of the
clip, so camera movement is visible rather than a single frame that flatters a
shot that pans away. Tiles are 360px wide with the source timecode burned in.

Orientation is corrected. iPhone clips carry rotation as a display-matrix flag
rather than in the pixels, and the **Native** column below is the resolution
after that flag is applied, which is the one anybody actually sees.

## This branch is public

`next-visit-site` is a public repo. This orphan branch keeps the mirror out of
`main` and off Vercel; it does **not** make any of it private. Anything here is
published permanently - deleting later does not unpublish while a commit
referencing the blob survives.

> ### The Faces column is `unreviewed`
>
> Nobody has looked yet. It does **not** mean the clips are clear of faces, and
> an unreviewed row must not be read as cleared. The hub does this pass.
>
> Flag a clip where a face is **identifiable and front-on**. People walking away
> from camera are fine and are not flagged.
>
> **Judge a borderline frame at full resolution, not off the sheet.** At tile
> size these sheets have previously read the back of a head as a face, and a man
> looking straight into the lens as an ambiguous profile. Both errors happened
> on the same batch.

## Renamed for collisions

Drive lets two files share a name and this library has several such pairs. At
least one of them - `IMG_1724` - is **two different takes rather than copies**,
so overwriting would have silently dropped footage. The first occurrence keeps
the plain basename and the rest take a numeric suffix. Names are assigned across
the whole library before batching, so they do not depend on where a batch
boundary fell.

## Phase 2

Name the clips you want and they get transcoded onto this branch under
`clips/`: h264, crf 18, preset medium, yuv420p, no audio, native aspect ratio,
longest side capped at 1920, **no pre-crop to 9:16** so the crop window stays
your choice per shot.
