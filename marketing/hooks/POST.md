# Anticipation hooks - post pack

Six 10s vertical clips, 1080x1920, silent (audio track stripped so a voice
memo can be laid over any of them). No product appears in any frame. That is
deliberate: the video poses the question, the pinned comment answers it.

**The thesis all six serve:** the anticipation is the best part. Having nothing
booked is the actual problem.

## How to post one

1. Post the clip with the caption below. Add a platform library track at post
   time (nothing is burned in, so the audio page ranking signal stays intact).
2. **Immediately pin your own comment** using the pinned line below. This is
   where the product gets named. Keeping it out of the video is what makes
   someone tap the profile.
3. Reply to early comments in the first hour. The question at the end of each
   caption is there to make that easy.

No emoji, no hype, no em dashes. Hashtags are deliberately light: the hook does
the work, and heavy travel tagging mis-signals the audience for this track.

## Judge these on

**Watch-through percentage and follows per 1,000 views.** Not raw views. Views
do not compound and are mostly distribution luck; a follower sees everything
afterwards. Link taps become the metric later, once there is traffic worth
converting.

## Notes

- Nothing in these captions cites a statistic that could be challenged in the
  comments. The eight weeks claim is the only research-backed line and it is
  phrased without a false precision number on purpose.
- Do not run two hooks from the same angle back to back. The two job ones
  (1 and 4) should sit at least three posts apart.
- If a hook lands, the follow up is not a sequel to that hook. It is the same
  thesis from a new angle, otherwise the account reads as a quote page.
